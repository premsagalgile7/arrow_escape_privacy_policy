1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to Arrow Escape 3D ("the Game", "we", "our"). We believe that great puzzle games should be fun, private, and secure. This Privacy Policy describes our privacy practices and confirms that Arrow Escape 3D does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

✔ Privacy by Design: Arrow Escape 3D is a completely offline, standalone puzzle game. You can play all 500 levels, daily challenges, and custom builder modes without creating an account, registering an email, or connecting to external servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To provide full transparency under the Google Play Console policies, we explicitly certify that we do NOT collect, access, or store:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, postal addresses, or user account credentials.
  • No Location Telemetry: We never request GPS, coarse network location, cell tower IDs, or Wi-Fi SSID telemetry.
  • No Financial Information: There are no paid microtransactions, credit card processing, or financial data handling.
  • No Device or Advertising IDs: We do not read Google Advertising ID (AAID), Android ID, IMEI, or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Photos, Media, or Files: We do not request storage permissions or access your camera, gallery, or microphone.

3. Android Device Permissions

Arrow Escape 3D adheres strictly to the principle of least privilege. We only declare the single normal permission required for tactile sensory feedback:

Permission Classification Usage & Purpose
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal (Install-time) Used exclusively to trigger tactile haptic pulses when launching arrows, bumping obstacles, or completing a puzzle.

Haptic feedback can be customized or disabled completely at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

4. On-Device Local Storage (Room Database)

All game state data resides strictly on your local device inside Android's protected application sandbox via Room SQLite database and SharedPreferences. This local data includes:

  • Level progress, unlocked worlds, and star ratings across all 500 campaign puzzles.
  • Local personal best leaderboard records (completion times and total moves per level).
  • Daily challenge streaks, unlocked achievement badges, and in-game star gems.
  • Audio volume, haptic intensity, and visual theme preferences.

This data never leaves your device and is not synchronized to any remote server.

5. Zero Third-Party Trackers & Ads

Arrow Escape 3D contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, analytics frameworks, or tracking pixels. There are no AdMob, Unity Ads, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDKs, or third-party cookies bundled in the game.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Arrow Escape 3D is designed for audiences of all ages, including children and families. Because the game does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it fully complies with:

  • COPPA: Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (United States).
  • GDPR / GDPR-K: General Data Protection Regulation (European Union).
  • Google Play Families Policy: Designed with zero behavioral profiling and child-safe content.

7. Google Play Console Data Safety Summary

Data Safety Questionnaire Answers for Google Play:

  • Data Collection: No user data is collected.
  • Data Sharing: No user data is shared with third parties.
  • Data in Transit: N/A (no network requests are made by the application).
  • Data Deletion: Users can delete all local data anytime via Android App Info → Clear Data.

8. Security & Data Deletion

Since all puzzle progress and scores reside exclusively in local storage, your data is protected by the Android OS application sandbox. You have full control over your data. To reset or delete all game progress:

Open Android Settings → Apps → Arrow Escape 3D → Storage → Clear Data or simply uninstall the application.
